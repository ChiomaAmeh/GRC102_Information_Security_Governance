# Task 4: Security Steering Committee

## Terms of Reference

### Purpose

Provide a formal governance forum for cross-functional security decision-making.

### Scope

- Security policy review
- Strategic security planning
- Resource prioritisation
- Incident oversight
- Conflict resolution
  
### Membership

- Director Information Security Governance (Chair)
- CEO
- CTO
- IT Manager
- Compliance Officer
- HR Manager
- Development Representative
- Finance Representative
  
### Responsibilities

- Review policy
- Oversee initiatives
- Resolve disputes
- Monitor security governance

### Meeting Cadence

Monthly and as required.

### Decision Authority

Consensus-driven where possible, with escalation to executive leadership and the Board when necessary.

### Reporting

Committee outcomes are documented and reported quarterly.

---

## First Meeting Agenda

1. Welcome and introductions
2. Adoption of Terms of Reference
3. Review of recent security near-misses
4. Password policy discussion
5. Governance blueprint review
6. Security metrics update
7. Other business
8. Meeting close

---

## CEO Briefing Note

The Security Steering Committee provides a structured governance forum for resolving conflicts between business and technical stakeholders.

In relation to the password policy dispute, the committee allows all stakeholders to present evidence-based arguments and reach decisions aligned with organisational objectives, risk appetite and industry best practice.

The recommended approach combines stronger authentication practices, multi-factor authentication and secure password management rather than frequent forced password changes.
