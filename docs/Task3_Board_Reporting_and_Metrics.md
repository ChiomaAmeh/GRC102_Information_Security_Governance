# Task 3: Board Reporting and Security Metrics

# Board Executive Summary

## Overall Security Posture

AMBER trending toward RED.

Four of five core metrics are moving in the wrong direction. While employee security awareness is improving, incident levels, phishing attacks and vulnerability backlogs continue to increase.

---

## Selected Security Metrics

| Metric | September | February | Trend |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| High-Risk Incidents | 2 | 7 | Increasing |
| Unpatched Critical Vulnerabilities | 5 | 18 | Increasing |
| Critical Vulnerability Patching Rate | 67% | 55% | Declining |
| Phishing Attempts | 150 | 320 | Increasing |
| Training Completion | 45% | 70% | Improving |

---

## Priority Risks

### Risk 1: Vulnerability Backlog

Critical vulnerabilities increased from 5 to 18 over six months.

### Risk 2: Phishing Threat Growth

Phishing attempts more than doubled from 150 to 320.

---

## Recommendations

### Recommendation 1

Implement a mandatory fourteen-day remediation target for critical vulnerabilities.

### Recommendation 2

Achieve 100% security awareness training completion and conduct routine phishing simulations.

---

## Why These Metrics Were Selected

These metrics provide direct visibility into organisational exposure, operational security performance, workforce preparedness and business risk. They support informed Board-level decision-making and governance oversight.
