# Task 5: Governance Maturity Assessment

## Current-State Assessment

| Domain | Level | Assessment |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Policy & Documentation | 2 | Inconsistent policies inherited from acquisitions |
| Roles & Responsibilities | 1 | Accountability is unclear |
| Risk Management | 1 | Reactive approach |
| Metrics & Reporting | 2 | Metrics exist but lack business focus |
| Training & Awareness | 2 | Mandatory training but low engagement |
| Compliance | 2 | Audit-driven rather than continuous |
 
---

## 12-18 Month Roadmap

### Initiative 1: Governance Accountability Framework

Objective:
Establish clear governance ownership.

Outcome:
Documented governance structure and RACI model.

---

### Initiative 2: Policy Harmonisation

Objective:
Create a unified governance framework.

Outcome:
Single approved policy library.

---

### Initiative 3: Formal Risk Management

Objective:
Implement risk-based governance practices.

Outcome:
Board-approved risk register and risk appetite statement.

---

### Initiative 4: Board Reporting Programme

Objective:
Establish routine governance reporting.

Outcome:
Quarterly Board-level security reporting.

---

### Initiative 5: Training and Compliance Modernisation

Objective:
Improve organisational awareness and compliance maturity.

Outcome:
Training participation greater than 90%.

---

## Executive Summary to the Board
GHC currently operates between Level 1 and Level 2 governance maturity across most assessed domains.

Implementation of the proposed roadmap will establish standardised governance processes, strengthen accountability, improve risk management, enable better executive decision-making and position the organisation to achieve at least Level 3 maturity within 12 to 18 months.
