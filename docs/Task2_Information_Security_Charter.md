# Task 2: Information Security Charter

## Purpose

This Charter establishes GHC's Information Security Programme as a standing business-aligned function. It protects patient information, business data and organisational assets while supporting growth, innovation, regulatory compliance and customer trust.

## Scope

The programme covers:

- All information assets
- Applications and systems
- Employees and contractors
- Third-party suppliers
- Acquired organisations
- Patient data
- Financial data
- Intellectual property
  
## Authority

The CEO issues the Charter, and the Board Risk & Audit Committee approves it.

The Director of Information Security Governance has authority to:

- Establish security policy
- Direct governance activities
- Require risk mitigation
- Escalate unacceptable risks to executive management and the Board
  
## Roles and Responsibilities

- Board: Sets risk appetite and provides oversight.
- CEO: Sponsors the security programme.
- Director IS Governance: Owns strategy and governance.
- Steering Committee: Provides cross-functional oversight.
- Department Managers: Implement approved policies.
  
## Key Principles

### Risk-Based

Security priorities must align with organisational risk.

### Business-Aligned

Security supports strategic business goals.

### Proportionate

Controls should match risk exposure.

### Continuous Improvement

Security governance must evolve with organisational needs.

## Reporting Structure

The Director of Information Security Governance reports operationally to the CEO and independently to the Board Risk and Audit Committee on a quarterly basis.

## Review and Approval

The Charter shall be reviewed annually and approved by executive leadership and the Board Risk and Audit Committee.
